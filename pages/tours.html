Lunar Colony Experience

$12,500 per person
Duration: 3 Days Group Size: 12 People Difficulty: Moderate

Experience life on humanity's first permanent extraterrestrial settlement. The Lunar Colony offers a unique opportunity to witness cutting-edge space habitation technology while enjoying breathtaking views of Earth from the Moon's surface.

This three-day expedition includes guided tours of the colony's facilities, low-gravity activities, and spectacular lunar landscape excursions.

Book This Tour

Tour Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival and Orientation

Spacecraft landing at Tranquility Base, welcome ceremony, safety briefing, and accommodation in the lunar habitat. Evening Earth-viewing from the observation dome.

Day 2: Colony Exploration

Guided tour of research facilities, agricultural domes, and manufacturing centers. Low-gravity sports activities and lunar rover excursion to nearby craters.

Day 3: Deep Space Observation & Departure

Visit to the lunar far-side radio telescope, final souvenir shopping at the colony boutique, and preparation for return journey to Earth.

Mars Base Alpha

$25,000 per person
Duration: 7 Days Group Size: 8 People Difficulty: Challenging

Journey to the Red Planet and explore humanity's foothold on Mars. Mars Base Alpha represents the pinnacle of interplanetary exploration and settlement technology.

This week-long adventure includes surface excursions, research participation opportunities, and the chance to witness Martian sunsets that paint the sky in hues of blue and pink.

Book This Tour

Tour Itinerary

Days 1-2: Journey to Mars

High-speed transit to Mars orbit, orbital acclimatization, and shuttle descent to Mars Base Alpha. Settlement orientation and accommodation assignment.

Days 3-5: Martian Exploration

Guided tours of Olympus Mons base camp, Valles Marineris canyon expeditions, and participation in ongoing research projects. Evening lectures from resident scientists.

Days 6-7: Polar Cap Expedition & Return

Excursion to the northern polar ice cap, underground habitat experience, and preparation for the return journey to Earth.
