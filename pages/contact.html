Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our tours and services

What are the physical requirements for space tours?

Most of our orbital tours require passengers to pass a basic medical examination. Longer duration tours to Mars or beyond have more stringent health requirements. Our team will guide you through the pre-travel health assessment process.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 3-6 months in advance for orbital tours and 6-12 months for interplanetary expeditions. Popular dates fill up quickly, so early booking is advised.

What safety measures are in place?

All our spacecraft and facilities exceed international safety standards. We employ redundant systems, extensive emergency training, and real-time monitoring. Our safety record is 100% incident-free since inception.
